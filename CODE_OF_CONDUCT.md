# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SSD Watcher should be a welcoming project for people of every background and
experience level. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected
to treat one another with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful impact.
- Welcome questions from people who are new to macOS or software development.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's paths, logs, credentials, or
  personal information.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, trolling, and publication of private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release
channels, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this
policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately,
and protect the person reporting a concern.

For a private conduct concern, contact the maintainer through a private contact
method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Takhoffman).
If that is not possible, use GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools. Do not open
a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant][covenant].

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/
